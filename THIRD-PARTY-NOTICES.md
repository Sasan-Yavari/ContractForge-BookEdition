### LocalStack 4.8 Community Edition: version-specific terms

The Compose dependency is pinned to the multi-platform registry manifest `sha256:8c9756bddc44625cd6e890f5e4bcc29b045eebe108b268482b848f4f832ec153`; `docker buildx imagetools inspect localstack/localstack:4.8` resolved this digest, and the locally used image reports the same RepoDigest and image ID. Its creation date is 2025-09-16. The corresponding upstream `v4.8.0` source release was published 2025-09-11 at commit `668c3f7df8733089274738f24cc8348fd811d532`. LocalStack's official Community Edition support article specifically confirms commercial use under Apache 2.0. These version-specific records resolve the commercial-use checkpoint for this pinned legacy Community distribution; the current Hobby-plan non-commercial terms describe the later consolidated offering, not this 4.8 image.

The 4.8 EULA still applies and must be reviewed/accepted by users. It states the software may communicate for updates, error reports, and anonymized usage data; this Compose configuration does not claim to disable those communications. The repository references and pulls the image directly and does not redistribute or mirror its layers. Keep the versioned Apache license, EULA, and third-party notices available through the links above; do not imply the project MIT license covers the image.

Maintenance limitation: LocalStack ended regular Community Edition support when it moved to the consolidated distribution on 2026-03-23; the legacy source repository was archived read-only. Its transition announcement says legacy versions will not receive regular updates/backport support. The pinned 4.8 image remains commercially usable under its Apache/EULA terms, but it has no regular product update, security backport, or maintenance guarantee. The project retains it because it is the verified local walkthrough dependency; updating it would require a separate compatibility and terms review. This is a disclosed maintenance risk, not a commercial-use license blocker.
